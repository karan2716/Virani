About India

India, officially known as the ROI, is the seventh-largest country by area and the most populous country in the world. It is a land of unmatched diversity, home to numerous languages, religions and traditions that have coexisted for thousands of years. The UNESCO has recognized more than 40 world heritage sites within India.

As the famous travel writer once said, “ India is not a country, it is a subcontinent of countries” — Travel & Leisure Magazine. India gained independence on 15th August, 1947, and since then it has grown into one of the most visited tourist destinations across the globe.

Quick Facts About India

  • Capital: New Delhi
  • National Animal: Royal bengal Tiger
  • National Bird: Peacock
  • Currency: Indian Rupee(INR)
  • Official Languages: Hindi and English (plus 21 other scheduled languages)
